# Ecommerce-Sales-Power-BI-Dashboard
Welcome to the eCommerce Sales Power BI Dashboard repository! This project provides a comprehensive and interactive dashboard to analyze and visualize sales data for eCommerce businesses. The dashboard leverages the power of Power BI to offer insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and more.
Features:
Sales Overview: Visualize total sales, average order value, and sales trends over time.
Product Performance: Analyze top-performing products, categories, and sales distributions.
Customer Insights: Understand customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and lifetime value.
Geographical Analysis: Map sales data by region to identify high-performing areas.
Real-time Updates: Integrate with live data sources for up-to-date analytics.
Customizable Reports: Modify and extend reports to fit specific business needs.
Use (Power BI tool)
Exploring Data: Use interactive visuals to explore different aspects of your sales data.
Filtering and Slicing: Apply filters and slicers to focus on specific time periods, products, or customer segments.
Exporting Reports: Export visuals and reports for presentations and detailed analysis.
